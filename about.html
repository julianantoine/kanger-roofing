About Kanger Roofing

Family-owned. Locally built.

We started with a ladder and a pickup truck.

In 2005, Mark Kanger hung his shingle — and his ladder — in a West Side driveway. Twenty years and more than 2,500 roofs later, the company is bigger, but the rule hasn't changed: do the job like it's your own house, because for one of us, it is.

We're a local crew, not a national franchise. That means the owner answers the phone, the people on your roof are our employees — not day-labor subs — and the warranty we sign is one we'll be here to honor.
