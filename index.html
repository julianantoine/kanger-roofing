Storm damage?

If your roof took a hit, your insurance may cover it.

Most homeowners don't know their policy covers storm and hail damage — or that they have a limited window to file. We inspect for free, document everything with photos, and walk your claim through with the insurance company.

  • Free damage inspection, anywhere in Greater Cincinnati
  • Photo documentation you can submit yourself
  • We meet your adjuster on the roof — with you
  • No payment until your claim is approved
Get a storm inspection →

Common signs of storm damage

  • Missing or displaced shingles
  • Granules collecting in gutters & downspouts
  • Dents in gutters, vents, or AC units
  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Shingles that look bruised or soft to the touch

See any of these? Call us — an inspection takes 30 minutes and costs nothing.
